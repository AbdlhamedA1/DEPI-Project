# 3. PROJECT OVERVIEW

The project is a student housing marketplace and management platform operating in Egypt.

The platform connects:

1. Students looking for accommodation.
2. Property owners and authorized property managers offering accommodation.

The platform is an intermediary technology company.

Its main purpose is to make finding student accommodation easier, more organized, more transparent, and more trustworthy.

It is not a traditional real estate agency.

It is not a property rental management company.

It does not directly rent properties, collect rent, manage tenants' stays, or determine rental durations.

The platform provides the technology and infrastructure that connect the two sides.

## Core business concept

Property owners register and publish their properties.

Students discover available accommodation through search and filters.

Students submit booking requests.

Students and property owners communicate through the platform.

They arrange viewings and negotiate independently.

When both parties reach an agreement, they confirm it through the platform.

The platform records this confirmation but does not become the landlord or manage the rental relationship.

## 5.8 Booking requests

A booking request represents serious interest in accommodation.

It does not automatically constitute a confirmed rental contract.

Confirmed decisions:

- Students submit requests for a specific property or available accommodation portion.
- A student can have up to three open booking requests simultaneously.
- Students may submit multiple requests.
- A student cannot have more than one confirmed booking.
- The property owner receives the request and decides whether to accept or reject it.
- The parties arrange the viewing independently.
- The platform does not manage the viewing or negotiate the rental terms.
- The student can cancel an unconfirmed request directly.
- The property owner is notified when the student cancels.
- The property owner can reject a request.
- Rejection does not require an explanation.
- The platform records cancellation history internally.
- The platform does not expose internal cancellation history publicly.

## 5.9 Agreement confirmation

The platform provides a mutual confirmation mechanism.

- Both parties must approve the agreement inside the platform.
- An optional agreement document may be attached.
- Confirmation records the agreement between the two parties.
- The platform is not responsible for managing the rental contract.
- No rent collection is required in the initial version.
- The platform does not manage rental duration or the tenant's stay.

After confirmation:
- The relevant booking request is closed.
- The relevant accommodation portion becomes unavailable.
- Only the booked portion is marked unavailable.
- Other portions of the same property remain available.
- Other pending requests for the same booked portion are automatically closed.
- The platform displays that the relevant accommodation portion has been booked.

Do not mark an entire building or apartment unavailable when only one room or bed is booked.

## 5.10 Messaging

- Every booking request has its own conversation.
- Conversations contain text messages only.
- The messaging system is connected to the relevant request.
- The conversation remains open permanently after the request has been created.
- Rejection or cancellation does not automatically close the conversation.
- Both parties can continue communicating.
- Sharing external contact details is allowed.
- Unread message counters are required.

Separate conversation status from booking request status.

Do not implement automatic conversation closure based on booking status.

## 5.11 Contact information

Immediately after creating a booking request, both parties can see:

- Name.
- Phone number.
- Email address.

Private verification documents must never be exposed to the other party.

The interface must clearly explain the data-sharing behavior and handle user consent appropriately.

## 5.12 Notifications

The first version supports:

- In-platform notifications.
- Phone notifications, subject to the approved implementation and available services.
- Notification history page.
- Unread notification state.

Do not assume a specific SMS, push notification, or OTP provider without approval.

## 5.13 Reviews

- Property owner reviews.
- Student reviews.

Reviews are available only after a confirmed booking.

Review format:
- Rating from 1 to 5.
- Optional text comment.
- Reviewer name and profile image visibility according to the agreed public profile information.
- The other party can submit one response.
- Administration reviews reported or inappropriate content.

A viewing without confirmed booking does not qualify for a review.

The exact timing and eligibility rules for student and property owner reviews should be documented carefully.

## 5.14 Reports and complaints

Both students and property owners can submit reports.

Reports are submitted through a dedicated reports page.

Reports are text-only in the first version.

Report statuses:

- Under review.
- Responded.
