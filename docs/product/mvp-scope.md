# Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope & Future Roadmap Matrix

## Egyptian Student Housing Platform _(منصة سكن الطلاب في مصر)_

---

## 1. Overview & Scoping Philosophy

In strict alignment with `app.txt` (Section 17: _MVP Priority_, Section 5: _Confirmed Business Decisions_, and Section 16: _Strict Working Rules_), the MVP is designed around a singular strategic core:

> **Connect Egyptian university students directly with verified property owners and managers for trusted accommodation discovery, transparent pricing, and structured mutual agreement confirmation.**

To ensure rapid pilot deployment, operational resilience, and legal clarity under the **"NEVER GUESS"** rule, features outside the validated transaction core are deliberately deferred to future roadmap phases.

## 4. Invariant Boundaries (What We Will Never Compromise)

1. **No Unauthorized Brokers**: We will never introduce an unverified or open public broker role. Every property must trace to a verified owner or legally authorized representative.
2. **No Fake Reviews**: Reviews will never be opened to non-confirmed users or drive-by browsers.
3. **No Hidden Costs**: We will never permit undisclosed fees; all rent and expenses must be explicitly itemized in EGP.
4. **No Rent Intermediation in MVP**: We will not handle civil lease agreements or collect monthly rent checks, maintaining our pure technology marketplace posture.
